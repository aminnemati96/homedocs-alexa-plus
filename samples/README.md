# Sample documents

Fictional household documents for testing uploads and for the demo video.
All companies, account numbers and people are made up.

Like the documents preloaded in the app, these pages show **dates relative to
today**: a small script (`rolling-dates.js`) fills them in when the page opens, so
the bill is always due soon and the warranties are still running. Make the PDF or
screenshot on the day you upload it; an old one will have old dates. Generated
PDFs and images are gitignored for that reason.

Turn each page into a test file:

- **PDF:** open the `.html` file in Edge or Chrome, press Ctrl+P, choose "Save as PDF".
- **Photo:** take a phone photo of the page on screen or printed (JPEG is fine),
  or use Win+Shift+S to save a PNG screenshot.

| File | Good questions to ask after uploading |
| --- | --- |
| `power-bill.html` | "How much is my power bill and when is it due?" "Why was it higher this month?" |
| `tenant-insurance.html` | "Is my bike covered if it's stolen?" "What's my deductible for water damage?" |
| `phone-plan-contract.html` | "What happens if I cancel my phone plan early?" "How much is roaming in the US?" |
| `vehicle-registration.html` | "When does my car registration expire?" "What do I need to renew it?" |
| `laptop-receipt.html` | "Is my laptop still under warranty?" "What if I spill coffee on it?" |

Dates, counted from the day you open the page: power bill due in 19 days, laptop
manufacturer warranty ends in 42 days, tenant insurance renews in 52 days,
registration expires in 57 days, phone contract ends in 98 days.
